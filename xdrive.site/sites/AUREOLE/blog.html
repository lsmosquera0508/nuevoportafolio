My Experience

Internship · January 2026

BYD LATAM

I completed my first corporate internship at BYD LATAM in Torres de las Américas, Punta Pacífica, Panama City. I applied my Excel skills to customer feedback and business records while learning how different teams support the daily operations of an international automotive company.

Customer Feedback & Excel

I organized survey data in Microsoft Excel and used pivot tables to summarize responses for Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis. I grouped respondents as promoters, passives, and detractors based on how likely they were to recommend the company.

Records & Administrative Support

I helped review and organize invoices, update databases, and classify files. I also assisted with inventory records and order requests.

Cross-Team Learning & Field Visit

I observed how reception and human resources handle internal requests and learned about logistics tasks such as coordinating hotel reservations. During a field visit, I saw how work outside the office connects with commercial activities and customer service.

What I Learned

The internship taught me how to turn survey responses into a summary a team can use. It also helped me become more organized, communicate across teams, and adapt to a new work environment.
